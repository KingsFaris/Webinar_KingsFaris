Session Agenda

What we will cover

  • Opening and stakes — Why supply chain risk has moved from operations to board-level strategy
  • CIPPS overview — A simple structure to diagnose where risk is born and where it compounds
  • Context — Geopolitics, trade realignment, corridor dependence, and the GCC/UAE position
  • Inputs — Scarce materials, Tier-N opacity, and the upstream map that matters most
  • Process — AI logistics, control towers, digital twins, and implementation failure modes
  • Product + Sustainability — Distributed quality, ESG controls, circularity, and resilience metrics
  • Practical models — Risk scoring, heatmaps, trigger indicators, and a 60-day action plan
  • Closing — Board-ready recommendations and discussion prompts

  • Worksheet 2 — Digital Twin Readiness Score Card
  • Worksheet 3 — Strategic Supply Chain Risk Assessment
60-Day Action Sprint
1
Days 1–14: Diagnose — Build the CIPPS risk register, identify stop-the-line items, review plant and project corridor exposure, and assess technical-data readiness.
2
Days 15–30: Pilot — Launch one practical use case each for upstream visibility, scheduling or shutdown decision support, and quality/traceability control.
3
Days 31–60: Govern — Create dashboard ownership, plant escalation thresholds, executive reviews, and a quarterly risk narrative for board and operations leadership.
